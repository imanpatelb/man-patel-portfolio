Privacy policy

Last updated 9 October 2026

This site is run by Man Patel, an individual trader. It collects as little as it can: only what you choose to send, plus the technical data any website receives.

Who is responsible

Man Patel. For anything about your data, email mptraderx.capital@gmail.com.

What is collected, and why

  • Enquiries. When you use the enquiry form, your name, email address and message are emailed to me so I can reply. They aren’t stored on this website.
  • Letters and notes. When you subscribe, your email address is sent to me and may be added to a mailing list held by my email provider, so I can send occasional letters and updates. You can unsubscribe at any time with the link in any email, or by emailing me.
  • Comments. Comments on posts run on Giscus, which uses GitHub Discussions. You sign in with GitHub to comment, and your comment is public. GitHub’s privacy policy applies to it.
  • Technical data. The hosting provider processes your IP address and browser details to deliver the site and keep it secure. The enquiry and subscribe forms also use your IP address briefly to limit spam. Fonts load from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google’s servers.
  • Visits. Page views are counted with Vercel Web Analytics, which uses no cookies. For each view it records the page, the referring site, approximate location (country, region and city), browser, operating system and device type. Visitors are counted using a temporary hash of the request that is discarded after 24 hours, so you can’t be identified or followed across other sites.

What isn’t

No advertising and no tracking cookies. Your personal data is never sold.

Track-record figures are fetched from Darwinex’s public pages, and index data from FRED, by this site’s server; nothing about you is sent to either. If you follow a link to Darwinex or GitHub, their own privacy policies apply there.

Stored in your browser

If you switch between the light and dark theme, your choice is saved in your browser’s local storage so it’s remembered next time. It never leaves your device.

Service providers

  • Vercel — hosts the site and counts visits.
  • Resend — sends the form emails and holds the mailing list.
  • Google — the inbox enquiries arrive in, and Google Fonts.
  • GitHub — comments, through Giscus, and where blog posts are stored.

These providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States.

How long it’s kept

Enquiries are kept as long as needed to reply and follow up, then deleted. Subscriber addresses are kept until you unsubscribe.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, have it corrected or deleted, or withdraw your consent to emails, by writing to mptraderx.capital@gmail.com. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

This site isn’t intended for anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published here with a new date.
