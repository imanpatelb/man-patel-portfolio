02

The approach

If it can’t be written down as a rule, it doesn’t belong in the book. Every entry, exit, and size is decided by the system — my job is to build it, test it honestly, and stay out of its way.

The portfolio runs three strategies chosen to behave differently from one another, so it leans on diversification rather than any single bet. Position sizes scale with realised volatility, risk per programme is capped, and the books are rebalanced on a fixed schedule.

The objective is steady, risk-controlled compounding with shallow drawdowns — and a record clean enough to stand on its own.

i

Rules over instinct

Fully automated execution. No discretionary overrides once a strategy is live.

ii

Risk before return

A hard, volatility-scaled risk budget per book, set before any position is taken.

iii

Honest measurement

Out-of-sample testing and a third-party record. The track is the proof, not the pitch.

03

Risk framework

01

Capped exposure

A hard risk limit per programme, sized to recent volatility so position size shrinks as markets get rougher.

02

Low correlation

Strategies are selected to draw down at different times, smoothing the combined equity curve.

03

Rules-based

No discretion in live execution. The system trades the plan, in good conditions and bad.
