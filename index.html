02

The approach

I trade by hand. A setup starts with market structure, gets checked against how related markets are moving, and is weighed against the macro calendar. Part of the process is fixed rules I follow every time; the rest is judgement, applied in context.

The book is multi-asset — equity indices, metals, energy and FX — and mixes two kinds of trade: trend following when a market is moving, mean reversion when it has stretched too far.

The objective is steady, risk-controlled compounding with shallow drawdowns — and a record clean enough to stand on its own.

i

Structure first

Market structure sets up the trade; cross-asset correlation and macro events confirm it or rule it out.

ii

Rules plus judgement

Fixed rules where the process allows them, discretion where the context decides.

iii

Honest measurement

Every trade on a third-party record. The track is the proof, not the pitch.

In development

I’m building algorithmic versions of the rule-based parts of this process. It isn’t live: the aim is to run it within the next one to two years, and nothing on this page reflects it yet.
