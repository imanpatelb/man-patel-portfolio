    02

    The approach

    I trade by hand. A setup starts with market structure, gets checked against how related markets are moving, and is weighed against the macro calendar. Part of the process is fixed rules I follow every time; the rest is judgement, applied in context.

    The book is multi-asset — equity indices, metals, energy and FX — and mixes two kinds of trade: trend following when a market is moving, mean reversion when it has stretched too far.

    The objective is steady, risk-controlled compounding with shallow drawdowns — and a record clean enough to stand on its own.

    i

    Structure first

    Market structure sets up the trade; cross-asset correlation and macro events confirm it or rule it out.

    ii

    Rules plus judgement

    Fixed rules where the process allows them, discretion where the context decides.

    iii

    Honest measurement

    Every trade on a third-party record. The track is the proof, not the pitch.

    In development

    I’m building algorithmic versions of the rule-based parts of this process. It isn’t live: the aim is to run it within the next one to two years, and nothing on this page reflects it yet.

    Questions

    How do I invest in KBAD?

    Through Darwinex. KBAD is a DARWIN: an index Darwinex builds from my trading and offers to investors on its platform. You open an account with Darwinex and invest in KBAD there; I never handle investors’ money. KBAD on Darwinex ↗

    Who looks after the money?

    Darwinex, which describes itself as “authorised and regulated as a Broker and Asset Manager by the FCA in the United Kingdom and by the CNMV in Spain”. Darwinex states that clients of its UK entity have deposit protection up to £85,000 under the FSCS, and that eligible clients of its Spanish entity are covered up to €100,000 by FOGAIN.

    What does it cost?

    Darwinex charges investors a management fee of 1.2% a year on actively invested equity, and a performance fee of 20% of profits, settled quarterly on a high-water mark basis. Every figure on this site is before those fees.

    How is risk managed?

    In two layers. Darwinex runs its own risk engine on KBAD, independently of me, targeting a maximum monthly VaR (95%) of 6.5%. Within that, my own process combines fixed rules with judgement and weighs every position against upcoming macro events.

    Can I check these numbers?

    Yes. They’re calculated from the daily quotes Darwinex publishes on KBAD’s public page, update automatically, and Darwinex records every trade. The DARWIN quote is an index that started at 100, so today’s 138.27 means +38.27% since inception.
